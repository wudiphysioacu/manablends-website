Our Story

How did the story begin?

In the serene, emerald landscapes of Aotearoa, where rolling hills meet the vast Pacific and the air hums with a pristine energy, a unique wellness journey was brewing.

Wudi, an Acupuncturist of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), comes from Taiwan, a country with vibrant tea culture and ancient herbal wisdom. From his earliest years, the fragrant aromas of simmering Chinese herbs and the profound philosophy of holistic well-being had woven themselves into the fabric of his being.

When Taiwanese tea culture meets New Zealand's Flora

As life's currents carried Wudi across the ocean to New Zealand, he found himself captivated by the beauty of this land, the crystal-clear rivers, and the towering, ancient forests. It was here, amidst this natural abundance, that a new spark ignited within him. He began to notice that the fascinating array of native New Zealand flora, plants with unique properties, resonated with the principles he had learned in Chinese medicine. He sensed a profound connection between the ancient remedies of his hometown and this land.

Driven by a deep passion for natural healing, Wudi embarked on a journey of discovery.

He ventured into the hinterland, seeking out small, sustainable farms nestled in the valleys and forging connections with the Māori community, the indigenous people of New Zealand. He learned from their ancestral knowledge of the land and native herbs.

With each encounter, Wudi's understanding deepened. He meticulously documented the properties of Harakeke (New Zealand flax), known for its soothing qualities; the invigorating Kawakawa, traditionally used for its medicinal benefits; and the antioxidant-rich Mānuka, famed for its honey and potent healing powers. He saw parallels with the herbs he had studied for years, recognizing similar energetic profiles and potential therapeutic applications.

When Tea is not only tea but also Treatment

Back in his clinic, Wudi began a new experiment. With years of clinical experience in TCM, he combined the knowledge of Chinese herbal pairings with his burgeoning understanding of New Zealand's native botanicals. He carefully adjusted ratios, brewed countless test batches, and patiently observed the subtle nuances of each blend.

The goal was not merely to create a medicinal concoction, but a delightful and accessible way for people to experience the healing power of nature. Wudi envisioned tea blends that were not only beneficial for well-being but also offered an elegant and enjoyable sensory experience. He paid meticulous attention to flavor profiles, aiming for a harmonious balance that would appeal to a modern palate while retaining the inherent goodness of the ingredients.

Now, Pure Picks TCM Herbal Blends is born.

Each blend was a testament to Wudi's dedication, a fusion of Taiwanese heritage and his deep connection to New Zealand.

As word of Wudi's exceptional teas began to spread, people were drawn not only to the subtle yet profound health benefits they experienced but also to the story behind the brand:

  • Dedication to purity
  • Respect for the land
  • Harmonious marriage of ancient wisdom

Mana Wellness Tea became more than just a beverage; it was an invitation to embrace a moment of mindful well-being, a gentle infusion of nature's healing energy into the everyday. Wudi's journey, from the bustling streets of Taiwan to the tranquil landscapes of New Zealand, had blossomed into a gift for all who sought a natural path to health and vitality.
